STACK Cybersecurity — Digital Presence

At STACK Cybersecurity I led the overhaul of the company's digital footprint as the sole in-house marketing hire, covering the website, SEO, marketing automation, blog content, and video production. A breakdown of each piece is below.

Website & SEO

I redesigned key pages of stackcyber.com in the company's PHP-based CMS and built SEO into the process, driving average Google search rankings from roughly 35 down to 7 for competitive cybersecurity keywords. I tracked performance across GA4, Google Search Console, LinkedIn, Meta, and YouTube to see what was actually working.

Marketing Automation

I built lead generation workflows in Microsoft Power Automate connecting intake forms to our CRM, handling prospects from first contact through campaign enrollment. I also trained and deployed an AI-powered website chatbot, refining its knowledge base and responses to match our brand voice.

Content & Blog Writing

As part of the marketing team, I researched trending cybersecurity and compliance topics, built outlines, and wrote draft content for the Insights section of stackcyber.com. Posts went through a collaborative edit process and were published under a single byline, our Chief Impact Officer, for consistency across the site.
